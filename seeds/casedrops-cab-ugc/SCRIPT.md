# Casedrops Cabernet UGC — A-roll script (two wines, US only)

Same presenter and kitchen as the Scarànto and Ode de Bellecôte ads, with the framing pulled back slightly so the counter is in shot. American accent throughout, steered by her American voice reference. Raw 9:16 A-roll only: the editors add the Decanter 94 card, b-roll and end card.

The two versions differ only in take 2. Take 1 has no bottle, so it's shot once and used for both.

| Take | Length | Seed | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8s | empty-handed | The $400 comparison wines (shared by both versions) |
| 2 | 17s | holding the bottle | She sets the bottle on the counter: rated as highly, same grapes, the winemaker, $102 a case |
| 3 | 18s | bottle on the counter | Top 1%, under $20, tasting notes |
| 4 | 16s | bottle on the counter | Free delivery, $60–80 elsewhere, $17 each, link below |

Pronunciation: Far Niente = far nee-EN-tay · Petrus = peh-TROOS · Morrissey = MOR-ih-see.

## Changes from the brief (approved)
- "6 for just $20 each" became "seventeen bucks each", because $102 ÷ 6 = $17. The earlier "under $20 a bottle" stays.
- "are all worth $400 each" became "go for up to four hundred dollars a bottle".
- "uses the same grapes, from [winemaker]" became "made from the same grapes, by [winemaker]".
- "equally highly rated" became "rated just as highly".
- Brand voice instead of an independent-creator voice: "we'll deliver it for free" and "Link's below" rather than "Casedrops will deliver" and "I'll put a link to their page".
- Dolum: "This Napa Cab" became "This California Cab", because its label says California, not Napa Valley. US labelling rules only allow "Napa" if the grapes are from there. Raptor & Crimson's label says Napa Valley, so it keeps "Napa Cab".
