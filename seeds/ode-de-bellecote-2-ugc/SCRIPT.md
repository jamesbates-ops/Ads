# Ode de Bellecôte (hooks format) UGC — A-roll script, UK and US

Second Ode de Bellecôte ad, in the same format as the Dolum (Casedrops) ad.
- **Presenter, kitchen and framing:** same as the Casedrops Cabernet and Dolum ads.
- **Seeds:** the empty-handed seed for the hooks, then her holding Ode, then Ode on the counter.
- **Bottle lighting:** dulled to match the room.
- **Two versions:**
  - UK: Winedrops, British accent, £.
  - US: Casedrops, American accent, $.
- **Footage:** raw 9:16 A-roll only. The editors add the b-roll, overlays, end cards and the Jonny clip.

Take 1 is **three alternative hooks**. Each opens a separate video, followed by takes 2–6.

## UK version (Winedrops, British accent)
**Hook 1**
> There are just four hours left to try your Châteauneuf for the festive season.

**Hook 2**
> There's only four hours until stock runs out on the sommelier's wine of the year.

**Hook 3**
> The clock is ticking, and you won't want to miss out on Châteauneuf this cheap.

**Take 2**
> This is Ode de Bellecôte. It's been likened to Beaucastel's Hommage à Jacques Perrin. But that wine is eight hundred and fifty quid… whereas this one? Under twenty-one quid.

**Take 3**
> It comes from the same region, using the same grapes and growing standards. That means galets roulés in the soil: little stones that act like mini radiators, soaking up the sun all day and warming the vines at night. But it's all in the ageing, and this wine gets extended ageing, which gives it this gorgeous smoky finish.

**Take 4**
> They're well known in France, but their name isn't famous enough yet to command the same price tag. In the glass you get black fruit, black pepper, wild herbs and a bit of leather. Critics have rated it ninety-two points and up, putting it firmly in the top two percent of wines in the world.

**Take 5**
> So really, it should be around eighty quid a bottle. But Winedrops don't settle for that. They buy in massive bulk, straight from the vineyard, so they're selling cases of this for over seventy percent off.

*[Jonny clip]*

**Take 6**
> They drop hand-picked deals every single day, but this one is too good to ignore. Don't wait around, get yours today.

## US version (Casedrops, American accent)

- **Take 2:** "…But that wine is eight hundred and fifty dollars… whereas this one? Under forty bucks."
- **Take 5:** "So really, it should be around a hundred and twenty dollars a bottle. But Casedrops doesn't settle for that. They buy in massive bulk, straight from the vineyard, so they're selling cases of this for over sixty-five percent off."

## Changes from the brief (approved)
- **"holiday season" became "festive season"** in the UK version.
- **Hook 3: "That clock" became "The clock"**, because it opens the video on its own.
- **The long tasting paragraph is split across takes 3 and 4**, because it was too long for one take.
- **Galets roulés:**
  - "promote mineral uptake from the soil" became "soaking up the sun all day and warming the vines at night".
  - That is what the stones are known for. The "mini radiators" image is kept.
- **"an even smokier finish" became "this gorgeous smoky finish"**, to avoid an unsupported comparison with Hommage.
- **"rated above 92 pts" became "rated it ninety-two points and up"**, because the scores used before were 93, 92 and 92.
- **US discount: "over 70% off" became "over sixty-five percent off".** Under $40 against $120 is 67% off. The UK figure holds: under £21 against £80 is 74% off.
- **Smaller wording changes:**
  - "Homage" became "Hommage".
  - "likened" became "It's been likened".
  - "And that means it should be" became "So really, it should be".
  - "crates" became "cases".
  - "platform gets hand-picked deals per day" became "They drop hand-picked deals every single day".
- **Company grammar:** the UK version says "Winedrops don't" and the US version says "Casedrops doesn't".
- **Label:** kept exactly as on the supplied packshot.
