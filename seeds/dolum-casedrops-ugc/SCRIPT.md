## Changes from the brief
- Hook 3: "That clock is ticking" became "The clock is ticking", because it now opens the video on its own.
- "this one I got for under $20" became "this one? Under twenty bucks". The presenter is AI-generated, so a first-person "I got it" reads as a customer testimonial from someone who doesn't exist, which the FTC's 2024 fake-testimonials rule covers.
- "They come from the same region" became "It's California Cab too". The Dolum label says California, not Napa, so "same region" right after the Napa names would imply Napa grapes.
- "formerly the head at the elite Stag's Leap" became "who used to head up winemaking at the elite Stag's Leap", for a more natural read aloud.
- "And that means it should be" became "So really, it should be", to avoid three "that means" in a row.
- "crates" became "cases", and "Casedrops don't" became "Casedrops doesn't" (American usage).
- "Their platform gets hand-picked deals per day" became "They drop hand-picked deals every single day".
- Ad-libs added: "Trust me", "this one? Under twenty bucks", "Mmm".

## To check before launch
- **"Four hours left" (hooks 1 and 2):** this has to be true whenever the ad runs, or it's a fake-countdown risk.
- **"Sommelier's wine of the year":** which sommelier or award? It needs a source you can cite.
- **"Likened to Far Niente, Opus One, Caymus":** by whom? Attribute it, or treat it as opinion.
- **"Those wines cost hundreds":** Caymus's regular Cab may be closer to $100, so check current US retail.
- **Chad Alexander:**
  - His actual title at Stag's Leap.
  - Which Stag's Leap he means: Stag's Leap Wine Cellars and Stags' Leap Winery are different wineries.
  - The Cabernet ad says he "used to make Silver Oak". Both claims need to be true.
- **"Over 70% off" against $60–80:** this holds at $17 a bottle (72–79%) but not at $19.99 against $60 (67%). The $60–80 must be a price other retailers really charge.
- **"Holiday season":** fine as long as it airs in November–December.
- **Jonny's "I've overordered":** this should be true.
- **End card:** 21+, "where legal", the ad label, and the AI-generated label that TikTok and Meta require.
- **Dolum packshot:** its quote line is garbled, so the seeds render Horace's "No poems can please for long or live that are written by water drinkers".
