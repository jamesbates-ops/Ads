# Scarànto UGC — A-roll script (UK and US versions)

9:16 talking-head A-roll in three takes. The editors cut b-roll in over and between them. One presenter, same kitchen as the seed, iPhone propped on the worktop, diegetic iPhone-mic audio (room tone, tile echo, glass knocks), no music.

Pronunciation: Scarànto = skah-RAHN-toh · Sassicaia = sass-ee-KAI-ah · Tignanello = teen-yah-NELL-oh.

| Take | Length | Seed frame | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|

Running time is about 52s of A-roll before b-roll.

## UK version (British accent)

**Take 1** (holding Sassicaia and Tignanello)
> Sassicaia... and Tignanello. Both of these? Over a hundred and fifty quid. A BOTTLE. *(incredulous little laugh)* Mad.

**Take 2** (holding Scarànto)
> And THIS is Scarànto. Same style, from the same region... but we'll do it for just a tenner. It's a hundred-point rated, just like Sassicaia, and just like Tignanello. But it's a fraction of the price. It's full-bodied, it's complex, and it's got these tannins that just melt in your mouth... same as the other two. Mm-hmm.

**Take 3** (holding Scarànto)
> Except you can get TWELVE of these for the price of one Sassicaia. Just in case you missed that: twelve bottles, for the price of one. This bottle is a flawless example of Italian winemaking... and a case of six is yours for under seventy quid. Come on!

## US version (American accent)

## Notes for sign-off
- The ad-libs ("Mad" / "Crazy", "Mm-hmm", "Come on!", the laugh, and repeating "twelve bottles, for the price of one") add personality only. They make no new product claims.
- US pricing: take 2 originally said "just ten", which didn't match six for under $120. The line was changed to "just twenty". At $20 a bottle, "twelve for the price of one Sassicaia" means a Sassicaia price of $240 or more, so check that holds for current US retail.
