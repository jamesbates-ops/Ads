# Ode de Bellecôte UGC — A-roll script (UK and US versions)

Same presenter, kitchen and setup as the Scarànto ad, holding the Ode de Bellecôte Châteauneuf-du-Pape. Raw 9:16 talking-head A-roll in three takes. The editors add the price cards, b-roll and end card in post. The iPhone is propped on the worktop and the audio is diegetic iPhone-mic sound with no music.

Pronunciation: Châteauneuf-du-Pape = shah-toh-NUHF doo PAHP · Jeb Dunnuck = DUN-uk · Jeff Leve = LEV · CellarTracker = "seller tracker". She never says "Ode de Bellecôte" aloud.

| Take | Length | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8s | Hook, then she points up to the space where the editors put the price cards (Rayas, Bonneau, Beaucastel) |
| 2 | 20s | Lifts the bottle: same bracket, the three scores, top 2%, the price |
| 3 | 16s | Don't wait, tasting notes, the price comparison, call to action |

## UK version (British accent)

**Take 1**
> Châteauneuf-du-Pape is world-leading. *[points up to the top of frame]* Just look at these. *[holds the point]*

On-screen cards (editors): Rayas 1978 £3,800 · Henri Bonneau Réserve des Célestins £900 · Beaucastel Hommage à Jacques Perrin £850

**Take 2**
> This one is in the same bracket, just without the name. Jeb Dunnuck gave it ninety-three, CellarTracker gave it ninety-two, Jeff Leve gave it ninety-two. Top two percent of wines anywhere in the world. Except we'll do it for twenty-one quid a bottle.

**Take 3**
> I wouldn't wait around. It's spicy, it's oaky, and it's got this long cherry finish that lasts well beyond its price point. Except it's not nine hundred quid, it's twenty-one. So grab yours today.

## US version (American accent)

**Take 1**
> Châteauneuf-du-Pape is world-leading. *[points up to the top of frame]* Just look at these. *[holds the point]*

On-screen cards (editors): Rayas 1978 $4,900 · Henri Bonneau Réserve des Célestins $1,400 · Beaucastel Hommage à Jacques Perrin $1,000

**Take 2**
> This one is in the same bracket, just without the name. Jeb Dunnuck gave it ninety-three, CellarTracker gave it ninety-two, Jeff Leve gave it ninety-two. Top two percent of wines anywhere in the world. Except we'll do it for under forty bucks a bottle.

**Take 3**
> I wouldn't wait around. It's spicy, it's oaky, and it's got this long cherry finish that lasts well beyond its price point. Except it's not a thousand dollars, it's under forty bucks a bottle. So grab yours today.

## Changes from the brief (approved)
- Added "Just look at these" so the pointing beat isn't dead air.
- UK: "it's not £1000" became "it's not nine hundred quid", because £1000 isn't one of the on-screen prices and would read as a reference price the wine is never sold at. US keeps "a thousand dollars", which matches Beaucastel on screen.
- The closing line became "So grab yours today", so "wait around" isn't said twice.
- Spoken phrasing: "this long cherry finish", "a bottle" rather than "per bottle", "quid" (UK) and "bucks" (US), and numbers said as words.

## To check before launch
- "Top 2% of wines anywhere in the world" needs a named source. If it's Vivino's ranking, attribute it.
- The 93 / 92 / 92 need to be for the vintage being sold. CellarTracker is a community average, not a critic.
- "Same bracket, just without the name" is a strong comparison with £850–£3,800 wines.
- The on-screen comparison prices need to be real current retail prices you can cite.
- "I wouldn't wait around" implies limited stock. Fine if that's true.
- The end card needs an age line: 18+ for the UK, 21+ for the US.
- The supplied packshot's small appellation line reads "Chateaunent-di Pape Contrellz". The seed renders the standard "Appellation Châteauneuf-du-Pape Contrôlée".
