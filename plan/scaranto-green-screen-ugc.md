Generated with **Kling 3.0** (standard mode, native audio), every clip seeded from the same start frame (`presenter_9x16.png`, the supplied photo centre-cropped from 1131×2000 to 1125×2000), so framing matches across cuts.

Voice brief: mature British man, Received Pronunciation, deep authoritative baritone; delivery deliberate and assured, not slow. Each clip has extra tail room so the editors can tighten it.
Diegetic audio: dry close-mic studio voice with faint treated-room tone. No music, no SFX.
No on-screen text was generated. B-roll, supers and pack shots go in during the edit.

Numbers are written out in the prompts ("seventy percent", "two hundred and ninety pounds") so the speech engine reads them correctly.
