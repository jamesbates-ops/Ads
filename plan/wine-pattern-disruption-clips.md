# Wine pattern-disruption clips (Instagram Reels)

Short scroll-stoppers, each a single continuous shot. All clips: 9:16, 1080×1920, 4–5s, **no audio**, hands only (no faces). Generated in Higgsfield with Seedance 2.5. Branded bottles were animated from a still opening frame built from the real bottle photo, so the main label branding stays accurate. Small print on the labels is not legible on close inspection, which is fine at Reel speed.

## Notes for the edit
- **Hammer:** the top breaks more like a chip than the whole neck flying off. The wine burst and the flood over the label still land.
- **Torch take 2:** the flame ignites about 0.8s in rather than on frame one. Trim the head if you want it to open on fire. A thin blank white strip (the top edge of a label, no text) sits at the bottom of the frame.
- **Ridge** was in the original brief, but no Ridge photo was supplied, so there is no Ridge clip yet.
