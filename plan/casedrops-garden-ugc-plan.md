# Casedrops — garden UGC ad: shot plan

Format: 9:16, 1080p, UGC-style. A man sits at a metal outdoor table in a garden and talks to camera like he's talking to a friend. The camera is steady but handheld, like an influencer filming on a phone. Apart from the opening insert, it plays as one continuous shot. Each clip starts on the last frame of the clip before it, so the joins match. Footage is for the editors to cut. There is no end card.

Speech is generated in each clip with lip sync. Clip 1's voice is the reference for every later clip, so the voice stays the same throughout.

## Seed images

- **A**: seated at the metal outdoor table, bottle standing on the table.
- **B**: same setup, bottle in his hand, label readable.

## Notes

- 0a and 0b are made as one 8s clip with a hard cut at about 3s. The editors can trim the insert to anywhere between 2 and 4s.
- Prompts write the numbers out in words ("eighty dollars", "twenty bucks", "six", "a hundred-point", "seventy percent") and spell the brand as "Case-drops" so it is pronounced correctly.
