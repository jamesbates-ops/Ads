# Frankie & Chris — phone-call ad: timestamped video plan

Format: single continuous POV shot from Frankie's eyes, square (1:1), in her LA hillside apartment the morning after a dinner party. She stays at the kitchen island / dining table. The phone is in her left hand on speaker and sways naturally with her arm. Chris is only ever heard.

Total running time: **01:41** of dialogue plus a 4-second end card, about **01:45**. No voices in this plan; timings come from the reference dialogue mix (Ken as Chris, Isabella as Frankie) so the cut lands the same length if voices are added later.

Timecodes are mm:ss.s. Each shot lists the still to use as its seed frame (files in `plan/frames/`).
