## Audio build

Video is rendered **silent**. No face is ever on screen, so there is no lip-sync
constraint and the dialogue is free voice-over — which buys exact control over
casting, pacing and the phone-line treatment.

- Frankie — ElevenLabs preset **Emily**, natural and close, 2.5:1 compression.
- Chris — ElevenLabs preset **Archie**, through a 300–3400 Hz band-pass with 3:1
  compression and +3 dB makeup: the phone-line EQ the brief asks for.
- Tempo trims where a read overran its slot: shots 3/4/8/9 at 1.05x, shot 6 at
  1.12x, shot 11 at 1.05x. Pitch-preserved and inaudible at conversational pace.
- Ring: two synthesised double-pulses plus a low vibration, 0:00.2–0:02.5, so the
  first line lands at 0:03 as briefed.
- Bed: filtered pink-noise room tone under the whole spot.
- Mastered to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.

## Known limitations

1. **The on-screen call timer is model-rendered, not composited.** Generative video
   does not draw small UI text reliably, so the readouts need checking shot by shot;
   any that drift want a tracked overlay in a finishing pass. This is the one thing
   the brief calls out as "the thing a viewer will notice", so it is worth a look
   before the spot ships.
2. **No end card.** Shot 13 holds ~2s of quiet tail for one, but brand assets were
   not supplied.
3. **No foley.** Plate and glass handling is silent under the room tone; a foley
   pass would add realism the brief implies but does not require.
