## Production notes

- Nobody drinks in the Verse 2 driving flashback (shots 16 to 19), and no shot links driving with wine.
- Every on-screen adult drinking is clearly well over 21 (Son early 40s, everyone else retirement age). The style stays grown-up, not a kids' cartoon.
- Any on-screen text that must be exact ("70% saved", the CTA, store badges, the legal line) goes on as an overlay in the edit rather than being generated.
- Assembly: trim each clip to its In/Out, cut on the lyric boundaries above, lay the song underneath, export 1080x1920.
