# Casedrops UGC ad: 9:16 shot plan

Format: **voiceover over B-roll**, with no lip sync. Romy's voiceover plays over short silent 9:16 clips of the woman from the seed image (Higgsfield media `d7653e47-e126-44d6-8a6e-45fe189b4777`). She sits at a table with a glass of red wine, writes in a notebook, scrolls her phone and sips now and then. Every clip is its own file, and the editors cut the B-roll to the voiceover.

Voice: a fun, young American woman. Chosen voice: **Romy** (Higgsfield preset `2e5f5f01-6d50-5335-a9ae-e8f81cd42342`) on the **ElevenLabs** engine (`text2speech_v2`, variant `elevenlabs`). The spoken words are the script verbatim; only the punctuation and capitals were changed to make the delivery livelier.

## Claims to check before release
- H1 says $80 to $20, which is 75% off. H2 says 70% off. If both refer to the same bottle, make the numbers agree.
- The price, free delivery, trade price and full refund claims all need to be true and substantiated for the market the ad runs in.
- The presenter is AI-generated but the voiceover speaks as a customer ("I got it for…"). Label the ad as a paid ad, and add the AI or actor disclosure that the platform and market require (FTC/ASA, and TikTok and Meta AI labels).
- Alcohol ad codes: the presenter must look clearly over 25.
