# Casedrops UGC ad: 9:16 shot plan

Same woman, set, light and framing as the seed image (Higgsfield media `d7653e47-e126-44d6-8a6e-45fe189b4777`). She talks straight to camera, selfie-style. Every clip is its own file, so the editors can pick one hook and splice it onto the body.

Voice: a fun, young American woman, with the same voice on every clip.

Durations assume a brisk UGC pace of about 2.8 words per second, plus roughly 0.5s of room at each end for the editors to cut on.

## Claims to check before release
- H1 says $80 to $20, which is 75% off. H2 says 70% off. If both refer to the same bottle, make the numbers agree.
- The price, free delivery, trade price and full refund claims all need to be true and substantiated for the market the ad runs in.
- The on-camera presenter is AI-generated but speaks as a customer ("I got it for…"). Label the ad as a paid ad, and add the AI or actor disclosure that the platform and market require (FTC/ASA, and TikTok and Meta AI labels).
- Alcohol ad codes: the presenter must look clearly over 25.
